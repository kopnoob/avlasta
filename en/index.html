Service desk for Norwegian municipalities

A service desk built to take the routine work off your hands

Avlasta is a Norwegian service desk under construction. The aim is for the system to sort, answer and in time carry out the everyday tickets, so your people have time for what needs a human. What exists today, you can try yourself.

Try the demo See what exists now

No sign-up. The demo shows Fjordvik municipality, which is fictional.

What you can try now, and what is coming

The five everyday things are in the demo: reporting an issue, inbox and ticket view, email in and out, answers before the ticket is sent, and suggestions from AI. The list is updated when something new reaches the demo.

  • Report an issue in the portalIn the demo now

    Employees report the issue from phone or computer with a short description, get a ticket number straight away and follow it under «My cases».

  • Answers before the ticket is sentIn the demo now

    The portal shows common answers from the knowledge base and known faults before the employee sends the ticket.

  • Inbox and ticket viewIn the demo now

    The agent sees new, own and waiting tickets with deadlines, replies to the requester or writes an internal note, and resolves the ticket.

  • Email in and outIn the demo now

    An email becomes a ticket in seconds, and replies go out as email. In the demo everything goes to a demo mailbox, so no email is sent out.

  • Suggestions from AIIn the demo now

    Suggested category, summary and reply in the ticket. The Claude language model makes them when AI answers, otherwise fixed rules do, and the label on the suggestion says which. The agent decides.

  • Reports for managementIn the demo now

    The manager view shows tickets in and resolved, deadlines, open tickets per queue and a weekly report.

  • Norwegian Bokmål, Nynorsk and EnglishIn the demo now

    The portal and the workspace work in all three languages.

  • Value statementPartly

    The manager view shows how much of the work Avlasta could have taken. How much time the system actually took off your hands is coming.

  • AI that carries out routine tasksComing

    Such as access and passwords. You see what will happen before it happens, and can undo afterwards.

  • Several municipalities in one serviceComing

    Own portal, own deadlines and own report per municipality, with shared queues.

Items marked «Partly» are only partly in the demo. Items marked «Coming» are not in the demo yet. Read how it fits together

Who it is for

Municipalities

Few IT staff, many workplaces, and users who need help in Bokmål or Nynorsk.

Inter-municipal IT services

Several municipalities in one service, each with its own portal and report. This is coming.

Businesses

A service desk with everything included in the price, with no modules or AI add-ons to buy.

Who is behind Avlasta

Avlasta is delivered by Commit AS, org. no. 934 427 688, a Norwegian company. Øystein Kopstad is the contact and is responsible for agreements and customer relations.

How we build

Avlasta is built by a team of AI agents under human ownership and responsibility. The agents write the code, test it and review each other's work. No new version reaches the demo until the tests pass and an agent other than the builder has reviewed it.

We say this plainly because a buyer should know. It is also why the product can be built broadly at a price that suits small municipalities.

Who is responsible

Commit AS is the contracting party, signs the agreements and answers for the service. A person is responsible for agreements, privacy and customer relations. The agents have no standing access to customer data.

Where we are

Avlasta is new and has no customers yet. The demo is open, and what we build reaches the demo as we go. Anything that does not exist yet is marked «Coming» on this website.

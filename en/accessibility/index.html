Accessibility statement

Commit AS, org. no. 934 427 688, is responsible for this website. The statement covers the Avlasta website, help centre and status page. The aim is that everyone can use them, whatever their abilities.

Status

The website is built to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. We have tested it ourselves and found no failures. It has not been tested by an independent party yet, so we do not claim conformance.

How we tested

  • Automated test with axe-core 4.14 of 40 pages in light and dark theme, on desktop and at phone width (375 and 320 pixels), 21 November 2026. Result: no issues.
  • Manual keyboard test: all links, buttons and form fields can be reached and used, and focus is visible.
  • Checks of contrast, heading levels, page language and text alternatives for images.
  • Viewing at 320 pixels wide and at 200 percent text size without the page scrolling sideways. Wide tables scroll on their own and can be reached and scrolled with the keyboard.

Known limitations

  • Screenshots of the demo are images with text alternatives. You can try the screens themselves in the demo.
  • Not all pages exist in Nynorsk and English yet.
  • The website has not been tested with a screen reader by an independent party.

The demo

The Avlasta demo is a separate service. It is built to WCAG 2.2 AA and tested automatically before every new version, but gets its own statement once it has been tested by a third party.

Report a barrier

If you have trouble using the website, email kopstad@commit.no. Tell us which page and what was difficult. We reply within five working days.

Supervision

The Norwegian Authority for Universal Design of ICT (Uutilsynet) supervises the requirements. You can complain to them at uutilsynet.no.

Statement last updated 21 November 2026. Last test: 21 November 2026.

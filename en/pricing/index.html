How the price works

  • The number of agents can go up or down every month. If you need fewer, you pay less.
  • The platform tier is fixed for the agreement period. It only changes on a merger or when a new organisation joins.
  • IT cooperations and service providers get one tier for all their users, and share agents across customers.

Partners

Partners receive 10 percent for a referral, 10 percent for 2 years when they run the implementation, and 20 percent for resale. Get in touch.

Questions and answers

What is a user served?

A person with an account who can report issues: employees, and pupils and students where the service desk helps them. The figure is read from your directory when the agreement starts and once a year.

What is a contributor?

Someone who works on assigned tasks, comments and approves, but does not own queues or reply to end users. Contributors are free.

Why not just a price per agent?

Because the aim is that you need fewer people for routine tickets. The price should not punish you when that works.

How do public bodies buy?

Below the procurement threshold you can buy directly. Above it, we deliver on your contract, such as the Norwegian SSA-L or SSA-sky, without reservations.

Can we try before we buy?

Yes. The demo is open to everyone without sign-up. As a pilot you get half price for a year and no lock-in.

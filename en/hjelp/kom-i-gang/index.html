Getting started with the demo

The demo shows Avlasta as it is today, with the fictional Fjordvik municipality. You do not need an account.

What you can try

You can be two people in the demo:

  • An employee of Fjordvik municipality, who reports an issue in the portal.
  • An agent in the IT service, who finds the ticket in the inbox, replies and resolves it.

How to start

  1. Open the demo and choose English at the top of the page or at the bottom of the menu.
  2. Choose to report an issue as an employee, or to open the workspace as an agent.
  3. As an agent you pick who to be, for example Ingrid Solberg in the Service desk team. All the people are fictional.

Before you start

Do not enter real names, email addresses or other personal data in the demo. What you write may be seen by other visitors, and the demo can be reset at any time.

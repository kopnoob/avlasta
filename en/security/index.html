Security and privacy

What we do, and how far we have come. Status per item rather than big words. Avlasta has no customers yet, and the demo only contains fictional data.

Where Avlasta will run

When Avlasta is used with real data, everything will run in Microsoft Azure in Norway and be stored there. AI runs in the EU through Amazon Bedrock. No data about customers or their employees will be processed outside the EEA.

PartProviderWhereWhat is processed
The app and its functionsMicrosoft AzureNorway (Norway East)Tickets, messages and settings while in use
Database and attachmentsMicrosoft AzureNorway (Norway East)Tickets, users, attachments and audit log
AIClaude by Anthropic via Amazon BedrockEU (Frankfurt)Ticket text when AI is used. Not used for training
BackupsMicrosoft AzureNorway (Norway West)Encrypted copy of the database
Outgoing emailMicrosoft AzureNorway or EUReplies and notifications to users
This website and status pageMicrosoft AzureEUNo personal data. We collect nothing

This is the plan and applies from the first pilot with real data. Today the demo runs in the USA with fictional data only, and AI in the demo goes directly to Anthropic in the USA. So do not enter real personal data there. Data and backups are stored in Norway; AI is processed in Frankfurt because Bedrock is not available in Norway.

AI and personal data

  • AI is switched on in the demo. The Claude language model from Anthropic suggests where the ticket belongs. When it does not answer, fixed rules make the suggestions, and the label on the suggestion says which.
  • In the demo, AI goes directly to Anthropic in the USA, with fictional data only. With real data, AI goes through Amazon Bedrock in the EU.
  • Your content is not to be used to train language models.
  • National ID numbers, passwords and card numbers are to be masked before text is sent to a language model.
  • AI is never to see more than the user it helps has access to.
  • AI is not to assess, rank or profile employees.

Agreements

Before real data is used, we sign a data processing agreement. Municipalities often use their own template, and we accept it. Our own drafts are in progress.

Who is behind it

Avlasta is built by a team of AI agents under human responsibility. Commit AS is the contracting party and answers for the service. The agents have no standing access to customer data, and nothing reaches the demo without passing the tests.

Report a vulnerability

If you have found a security issue, email kopstad@commit.no. Describe what you found and how to reproduce it. We confirm that we have received it, and tell you when it is fixed.

The contact is also in security.txt. Please do not test in ways that could harm the service or other visitors.
